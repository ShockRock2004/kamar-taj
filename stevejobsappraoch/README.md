> "Innovation is saying no to a thousand things."

Your deck has forty words on every slide. Your product has eleven features and no headline. You are going to rehearse it once in the car on the way there.

This skill runs your work through the way Steve Jobs actually did it. Not the turtleneck. The process.

First it makes the thing worth presenting. It starts from what the customer gets and works backwards to the tech. It lists everything you are trying to do and cuts it down to three, the way Jobs did at his Top 100 retreat. It draws the 2x2 that took Apple from about forty products to four. It counts your clicks. It finds the parts nobody looks at and holds them to the same bar anyway.

Then it puts it on stage. A one line headline that fits in a tweet. A villain, usually the problem, before the hero shows up. Three parts said out loud. Benefits instead of features and numbers with context. Slides with almost no words. One holy smokes moment scripted to the line. A change of pace every ten minutes. A demo with a backup and a recovery line. One more thing only if it actually earns it. And a rehearsal plan, because Jobs rehearsed for two full days before every keynote.

It ends with a blunt review. One line verdict. The single biggest problem. What to cut. Before and after rewrites. A scorecard. It is hard on the work and decent to you.

It does not cosplay. No fake quotes. Everything it attributes to Jobs is sourced in references.md.

## Install

It comes in the main kamar-taj bundle at https://github.com/ShockRock2004/kamar-taj
or you can just grab this single folder.

```bash
cp -R stevejobsappraoch ~/.claude/skills/
```

Restart Claude Code and run /stevejobsappraoch on a deck, a product, a pitch or an essay. Find the full method in SKILL.md.
